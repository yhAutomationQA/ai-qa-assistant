## Patterns We Keep Seeing

- AI is good at generating test structure from structured input (tables, Gherkin). It's bad at inferring implicit behavior.
- Flaky test classification works better when you give the AI both the source and the CI log together — one without the other leads to wrong conclusions.
- Reviewing AI-generated tests takes about the same time as writing them from scratch, but catches different kinds of mistakes. Net benefit is marginal unless the test is formulaic.
